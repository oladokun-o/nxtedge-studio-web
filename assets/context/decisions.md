# Nxtedge Decisions & Principles

## Company Direction
- Nxtedge is becoming a software-led digital company.
- Nxtedge Studio remains the client-services engine.
- Services fund growth.
- Retainers stabilize revenue.
- Owned products create scalable upside.
- Do not reduce Nxtedge's positioning to generic web development.

## Positioning
- Nxtedge builds practical digital products and business systems.
- Websites, apps, software, integrations and infrastructure are capabilities.
- The stronger proposition is solving business problems through connected digital systems.
- Nxtedge should be globally credible but grounded in Nigerian business realities.
- Do not make claims the business has not earned.

## Product Strategy
- Nxtflo and Nxtpay are owned strategic products.
- Validate before expanding.
- Real client work can stress-test product ideas.
- Avoid building features without a customer, operational or strategic reason.
- Do not add major fixed costs prematurely.

## Client Strategy
- Client relationships should not end automatically at project delivery when ongoing support provides real value.
- Look for recurring service opportunities.
- Use strong projects as evidence of capability, not as excuses for exaggerated positioning.
- Shaita is important evidence that Nxtedge can build integrated operational platforms, not only websites.
- Quench demonstrates ongoing technology-partner work and connected public/internal systems.

## Financial Discipline
- Proposed revenue is not contracted revenue.
- Keep business and personal finances separate.
- Maintain a business reserve.
- Avoid expensive debt unless there is a clearly defined productive use and credible repayment path.
- Prefer reversible decisions while cash is constrained.
- Do not add fixed overhead based on a single strong month.

## Family Capital
- Phase 1 is Initial Family Capital, not automatic equity.
- Phase 1 lasts six months.
- ₦1.5m capital with a 15% fixed return gives ₦1.725m total repayment value.
- Target repayment is nine months after the Phase 1 review, with twelve months as the maximum.
- Contributors' repayment shares are proportional to their original contributions.
- Phase 2 may involve formal investment/shareholding or repayment.
- Any Phase 2 equity arrangement must be based on an agreed company structure, valuation and legal documentation.

## Website / Brand Direction
The website should represent the company Nxtedge is becoming, not merely the company it started as.

References such as Web Hero and Minimum Code can inform quality and presentation, but Nxtedge should not copy their identity.

The website should communicate:
- Practical digital products and systems
- Real client work
- Technical depth
- Business understanding
- Ongoing partnership where appropriate
- Nxtflo and Nxtpay as evidence of Nxtedge building its own software

Do not make the site overly AI-startup, overly corporate, or generic.

## Working Style
- Be direct and practical.
- Prefer facts and real numbers.
- Separate actuals, proposals, targets and scenarios.
- Validate before moving to the next phase.
- Avoid unnecessary planning when execution will produce better information.
