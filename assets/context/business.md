# Nxtedge Business Context

## Business Model
Services → recurring client relationships → owned software products.

Services currently generate cash and fund growth. Retainers make revenue more predictable. Owned products create scalable upside.

The long-term goal is a software-led company rather than a business dependent entirely on one-off projects.

## Current Direction
Nxtedge is moving from primarily project-based freelance/client work toward a structured software company with profitable client services, recurring support/retainer relationships, and proprietary SaaS/software products.

Services remain important while products are validated.

## Revenue Principles
- Do not treat proposed revenue as contracted revenue.
- Protect recurring retainers.
- Convert suitable project relationships into recurring support where there is real value.
- Prefer reusable/productizable work over custom work that cannot compound.
- Avoid adding fixed overhead because of one strong month.
- Product investment should follow validation.
- Keep major decisions reversible while cash is constrained.

## Phase 1 Family Capital
- Initial Family Capital: ₦1,500,000
- Phase 1 duration: 6 months
- Fixed return: 15% = ₦225,000
- Total repayment value: ₦1,725,000
- Repayment target: 9 months
- Maximum repayment period: 12 months
- Repayment begins after the six-month Phase 1 review
- Minimum business reserve: ₦300,000
- Early repayment: no penalty proposed
- No Phase 1 equity

The capital is intended to strengthen operations, sales, infrastructure, recurring revenue, and product validation.

## Phase 2
After Phase 1, actual business performance determines the next step.

Two broad paths:
1. Repay the Phase 1 capital plus agreed return.
2. A contributor may choose to roll relevant capital into a formal Phase 2 investment.

Phase 1 does not automatically create ownership or voting rights. Phase 2 would require a formal company structure, valuation, investment terms, shareholding and legal documentation.

## Optimistic Phase 1 Scenario
If execution goes well:
- Stronger recurring client relationships
- More predictable service revenue
- Nxtpay's first paying implementation and subscription revenue
- Nxtflo validated with early users
- Stronger sales pipeline and financial visibility
- A maintained ₦300k reserve
- A stronger basis for either repayment or formal Phase 2 investment

These are planning scenarios, not guarantees.

## Financial Discipline
Prioritize:
1. Record/collect revenue
2. Pay essential operating costs
3. Maintain tax/compliance provisions
4. Protect working capital and reserve
5. Invest in validated products
6. Introduce predictable founder compensation when sustainable
7. Retain profit for growth

Personal housing is not part of the current business funding plan.
