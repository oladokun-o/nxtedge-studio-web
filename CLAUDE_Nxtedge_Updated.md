## 1. What Nxtedge Is

**Nxtedge Digital Solutions** is the legal company.

**Nxtedge Studio** is the client-facing services brand.

Nxtedge is becoming a **software-led digital company that helps businesses turn ideas, processes and operational needs into practical digital products and systems, while building its own software products alongside client work.**

Nxtedge is not being built as a generic web-development agency.

The company currently combines:

1. **Client services** through Nxtedge Studio
2. **Recurring client relationships and retainers**
3. **Owned software products** such as Nxtflo and Nxtpay

Services currently fund growth. Retainers make revenue more predictable. Owned products provide scalable upside.

The transition is deliberate. Do not assume the business has already become a mature SaaS company.

## 3. Business Direction

The intended business architecture is:

**Services → recurring client relationships → owned software products**

### Services

Client work remains the current cash engine. Strong client projects should also provide:

- Revenue
- Case studies
- Technical experience
- Reusable patterns
- Insight into recurring business problems
- Potential opportunities for productisation

### Retainers

Recurring relationships are strategically important because they make revenue more predictable.

Do not assume a project automatically becomes a retainer. Recurring work should exist because there is a legitimate ongoing need and clear value.

### Products

Nxtflo and Nxtpay are owned product opportunities.

Products should be validated before major expansion, fixed overhead or aggressive spending.

## 5. Nxtflo

Nxtflo is an AI-powered agency/client-operations product being built by Nxtedge.

Long-term goal: a SaaS product for freelancers, independent professionals, studios and agencies.

### Core idea

Turn a new client into a project-ready client without endless calls, forms and back-and-forth.

### Core workflow

**Client intake → structured brief → proposal → e-signature → project handoff/delivery**

### Current product phases

- **Phase 1:** Client onboarding/questionnaire — complete
- **Phase 2:** AI structuring engine / Claude API brief generation — next/in progress
- **Phase 3:** Proposal generation, e-signature and PDF — defined
- **Phase 4:** Project delivery/reporting — not yet detailed

### Product principles

- Step-by-step UX instead of long single-page forms
- Save/resume progress
- Branching questions where useful
- AI-generated structured project brief
- WhatsApp and email as important communication channels
- Use real Nxtedge client projects as stress tests
- Validate before expanding

### Working pricing hypothesis

- Free: ₦0
- Solo: ₦5,000/month or ₦50,000/year
- Studio: ₦15,000/month or ₦150,000/year
- Agency: ₦35,000/month or ₦350,000/year

International reference pricing considered: approximately $9/$29/$79.

Pricing is a working hypothesis and is not final.

### Strategic role

Nxtflo is not only a future standalone SaaS product.

Long term, Nxtflo can become part of Nxtedge's own client acquisition and delivery workflow, with the website potentially acting as the front door to the onboarding system.

## 6. Nxtpay

Nxtpay is Nxtedge's payroll product opportunity.

### Working commercial model

- ₦500,000 one-time implementation
- ₦900/employee/month

### Initial MVP

- Employee records
- Payroll inputs
- Payroll calculations and rules
- Payroll runs and approval
- Payslips and reports
- Secure access
- Auditability
- Customer account/subscription management
- Multi-tenant reusable architecture

### Product strategy

The first customer should function as a design partner.

The next customer should be configurable without requiring a bespoke rebuild.

### Go/no-go criteria

Nxtpay should continue when:

- The customer pays the implementation
- Subscription economics are sustainable
- Support is manageable
- The implementation is reusable
- Additional customers can be acquired without proportional delivery effort

Nxtpay is a strategic product opportunity, not yet a mature product business.

## 13. Core Strategic Principles

### Services fund growth
Do not prematurely abandon profitable client services.

### Retainers create stability
Recurring revenue is strategically valuable, but only when the relationship and economics make sense.

### Products create leverage
Nxtflo and Nxtpay should eventually reduce dependence on proportional client-service hours.

### Validate before expanding
Use actual customer behaviour, payments, retention and support burden to decide what deserves further investment.

### Keep costs controlled
Do not add fixed overhead because of one strong month.

### Proposed is not contracted
Always distinguish:
- Actual
- Confirmed
- Proposed
- Target
- Illustrative scenario

### Build for reuse
When solving a recurring problem, look for reusable architecture, workflows and product opportunities.

### Real clients are stress tests
Client projects are a source of evidence and learning, not merely revenue.

### Execution over endless planning
Planning should establish direction and guardrails. When further planning will not reduce uncertainty, execute and learn from reality.

### Nigerian context matters
Design decisions should account for Nigerian operating realities, including payments, connectivity, WhatsApp, local business workflows and practical constraints, while keeping products internationally usable where appropriate.
